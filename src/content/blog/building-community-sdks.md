## Why SDKs

External APIs are often powerful but uneven. A good SDK turns that uneven surface into something teams can trust: typed requests, predictable errors, documented examples, and a release cadence that respects real projects.

Community SDKs is my place to collect those wrappers and integrations. Each package starts with one sharp question: what would make this service easier to adopt in a production codebase?

## Principles

- Keep the client small and understandable.
- Document the happy path and the awkward edge cases.
- Make framework integrations feel native without hiding the underlying API.
- Ship examples early so usage drives the shape of the package.

## What Comes Next

The goal is a library of practical SDKs with consistent documentation, release habits, and integration packages for the ecosystems where developers actually work.
